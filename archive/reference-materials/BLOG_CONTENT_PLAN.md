## 5) Template SEO cho moi bai
- Do dai bai: 1200-1800 tu
- Cau truc:
  - H1 (co tu khoa chinh)
  - Mo bai: neu van de thuc te nguoi dung gap
  - H2/H3: 3-5 muc chinh co checklist/bang so sanh
  - FAQ: 3-5 cau hoi
  - CTA cuoi bai:
    - Xem goi phu hop (`tim-goi-cuoc.html`)
    - Xem bang gia (`bang-gia.html`)
    - Goi 0822 036 382
- Lien ket noi bo:
  - 1 link ve pillar page
  - 2-3 link sang bai cung cum
  - 1 link sang trang chuyen doi

## 6) Quy trinh xuat ban moi bai
1. Nghien cuu keyword + intent
2. Viet outline H2/H3
3. Viet nhap lan 1 (tap trung giai quyet van de)
4. On-page SEO:
  - Title 55-60 ky tu
  - Meta description 140-160 ky tu
  - URL ngan, co keyword
  - Alt text hinh anh
5. Gan CTA + schema Article/FAQ (neu co)
6. Dang bai + kiem tra mobile + toc do tai

## 7) KPI theo doi hang thang
- So bai dang dung lich: >= 8 bai/thang (giai doan dau)
- Organic sessions vao blog: +20% thang
- CTR tu blog sang `tim-goi-cuoc.html`: >= 4%
- Tyle chuyen doi lead tu blog: >= 1.5%
- Top 10 keyword dat duoc sau 8-12 tuan: 20-30 keyword
